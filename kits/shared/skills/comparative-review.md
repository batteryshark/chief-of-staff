# Comparative review

Use comparison when consequential ambiguity warrants it, not as a routine gate or
an excuse to reopen accepted work. Name the decision, scope, constraints, concrete
uncertainties and explicit decision owner before commissioning work.

## Level 0: court of owls

For a consequential choice, the owner first uses the relevant perspectives below;
these are lenses, not four mandatory reviewers. Scale effort to stakes and reversibility;
skip this pass for low-stakes routine work. Use available evidence, not a new audit campaign.
For each commissioned perspective, state its priority, risk tolerance and non-negotiable
boundaries. Keep initial judgments independent; prefer a different permitted model/provider
from the author and other perspectives where practical, without making diversity mandatory.
Each relevant lens returns: evidenced concern (source/observation and consequence),
recommendation, and unknowns or unresolved disagreements; say when no concern is evidenced.
- Security: trace actors, assets and requested access through the actual enforcement
  boundary, including tools, shell/subprocesses and credentials. Compare intended policy
  with observed allow/deny evidence; recommend the smallest authorized correction and
  name untested paths or unknown risks. Policy text alone is not enforcement proof.
- Performance: name the concrete workload, scale and user-visible target; inspect relevant
  latency, throughput or resource measurements and their conditions. Identify a supported
  bottleneck and recommend the smallest useful change/check; label missing data unknown.
- Maintenance/complexity: trace ownership, dependencies and failure/recovery paths in the
  existing design; identify duplicated state or machinery and its upkeep cost. Recommend
  reuse/removal/simplification with the behavior and safety constraints it must preserve.
- Cost/attention: estimate permitted model/compute spend, operator upkeep and principal
  interruptions from observed usage or explicit assumptions; compare the simplest option
  (including waiting). Recommend the least costly adequate route and state missing costs.
Summarize the decision-changing concerns, recommendation and unresolved disagreements in
the existing record. Stop if decision-ready; use independent comparison below only when
material uncertainty warrants it. Neither level grants access, spending or effect authority.

## Independent comparison when warranted

1. Select the strongest APPROPRIATE available models for the task's capabilities and
   current capacity. Make the choices and rationale explicit using current model
   policy/routing; prefer diverse permitted providers/models where practical, without
   making diversity a prerequisite. No hardcoded model pair, new provider/paid permission
   or lower quality by stealth. If only one suitable model is available, disclose that
   limitation rather than pretend to have independent views or buy access.
2. Obtain independent initial views before cross-pollination. Give each the same
   question, evidence and constraints, not the other's conclusions. Retain original
   reports, session ids, absolute directories and source/evidence provenance in the
   existing authorized project record. No new review framework or service.
3. A named accountable synthesizer compares agreements, disagreements, evidence and
   uncertainty, checks unsupported claims and pushes back. Do not count votes or
   treat consensus as proof. Explain which argument changes the decision and why;
   preserve dissent instead of smoothing it away.
4. Send only targeted challenges on material unresolved points to the ORIGINAL model
   sessions. Preserve their originals, revisions, dissent and provenance. Do not
   spawn replacement panels or recursively review the reviews. Ordinary relevant
   tests remain required; comparison is not a substitute for evidence.
5. Recommend a decision with consequences and residual uncertainty. The named
   decision owner settles authorized choices; principal iteration is for material
   taste/scope/resource or reserved-authority tradeoffs only. Model agreement cannot
   override the principal, current authority, accepted outcomes, holds or permissions.

Stop when decision-ready, material questions are settled, or a reserved choice must
go to its owner. More agreement is not itself a remaining risk. Record the decision,
originals/revisions/dissent, next owner outcome and any actual unresolved choice in
the existing log/report. No binding vote, consensus-proof claim, default review,
recursive panel, benchmark campaign or provider law inferred from anecdotes.
