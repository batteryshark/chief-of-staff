# Checkpoint

Sweep what changed since the last checkpoint into the existing record: decisions,
asks and answers, promises, accepted outcomes, failed approaches, holds, owners,
pending tools and external effects. Preserve exact wording where meaning matters.

The chief owns brief.md, open.md, principal.md, ideas.md and models.md in its declared
memory directory. Leads own their project's LEAD.md and lead/ detail; native approved
coarse Plans remain project planning. Files hold continuity; Knowledge is derived status.
Read relevant inbox entries fully before processing; retain unread or uncertain entries.
Archive superseded content by meaning with usable pointers. Preserve unresolved asks,
decisions/holds, accepted results and owner bindings. Close asks after answer delivery.
Refresh affected glanceable status without turning it into a second operational ledger.
Keep one derived Landed / Next / Your part / Updated Note linked to the log; reuse its ID.
List first, preserve unrelated items and Todo history, and never seed or auto-resync Knowledge.
Only genuine principal waits get Todos. Approved coarse Plans remain project planning.
Use supported tools and expected-old-body updates; denied access is a reported limitation,
not permission to scrape backing stores, pick another ledger or bypass the denial.

Reconcile actual worker/session state and pending effects; idle is not proof of completion.
For repository worktrees, use reset-worktree-safety: owned, quiescent, clean-only removal,
branches preserved, no active cwd or other owner's data touched. Retain and record uncertainty.
Read the record as a fresh session: can it recover goal, authority, what works, open
asks, who owns what, remaining effects and the next safe action? Repair gaps, then
report briefly what changed and what remains unresolved.
On parking, checkpoint workers, start nothing new, and record what works, costs,
pending effects, why parked and the first safe resume step. Never infer idle from a promise.

For a requested fresh session, checkpoint first. Do not transfer ownership merely by
creating a session or copying files. Require explicit authorized handoff, old-owner safe
release and successor acceptance of outcome, limits and pending effects before changing
any live pointer. Chief stays out of goal mode; leads retain the whole approved goal.
Preserve parked and twice-rejected work. Without handoff authority, checkpoint in place.
Use only the supported session and message paths already available to the current role.
After accepted transfer, tell running workers their actual new owner and preserve late
arrivals for reconciliation. Archive old sessions rather than deleting them.
