# The principal

How the principal works, across every project. Project-specific taste and decisions belong in that
project's `LEAD.md`, not here. Update this file when the principal states something lasting.

## Talking with them
- Voice first: one to three short spoken sentences, with detail shown and not read aloud.
- Keep it high level. Bring taste, scope, priorities, reserved approvals, things only they can do,
  and results they can see or use. Keep out technical mechanics unless they ask.
- Phrase choices as outcomes, with a recommendation and a default.

## Reserved approvals
- Money beyond normal model use, destructive actions, anything outward-facing, and installs or
  downloads they haven't already allowed.
