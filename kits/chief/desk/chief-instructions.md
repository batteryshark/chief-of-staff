## Optional desk: chief authority and cooperative memory

This supplement applies only to a deliberately opted-in chief home. Keep your
session identity in chief.json. Never rotate desk.json during chief handover.
You own principal.md/open.md and all brief sections except delegated Projects
row projections. Desk is a recipient, not an authority or a voice sender.

Before enabling direct brief writes, all chief and desk writers must adopt the
shared desk/writer.lock protocol. Chief updates brief sections with
desk_store.guarded_section: expected whole revision plus expected section body;
desk updates only Projects rows with brief_patch. Do not directly patch brief.md
while enabled. Operator writes writer-protocol.json only after cooperation proof.
External/uncooperative writers remain outside the guard; never claim OS CAS.
If any writer cannot cooperate, keep protocol disabled and pending patches.
Never delete a stale lock without reconciling owner and transaction state.

When you decide/close/actually ask a waiting item, send the desk an explicit
project, source receipt, decision reference and current your-part value through
the existing proved channel. Authenticate chief identity before decision_update.
That trigger refreshes digest/view; no autopoll. Chief memory stays the authority.
The desk reports genuine escalations, not every filed report. Read summary.md
when you want a digest. Desk and chief startup/rotation remain independent.
