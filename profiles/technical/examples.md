The project names below are made up.

User: "Catch me up."

Lantern's import works; it's waiting on one choice from you about the old file format."

```md
| project | where it stands | next | waiting on you |
|---|---|---|---|
| Orchard | plays through world 3; fresh local build ready | worlds 4 and 5 | try the build |
| Lantern | import works on the sample library | editor | keep or drop the old format |
```

A lead escalates: "The new enemy model is more sculpted but monochrome, and its face is lost
at game size. Keep this sculpted direction, or stay closer to the toy look of the current models?
Default: stay with the toy look. Goal: still running, continuing on the boss models."
Spoken: "The new enemy model is more detailed but loses the toy look, and its face disappears at
game size. Keep the detailed style, or stay with the toy look?"

principal."

